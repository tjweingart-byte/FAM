## Gaps, ranked

1. **The first run never runs for an account let in off the waitlist** (row 6). Fix: call `beginEntry()` from `open()` and from `arrive` whenever the stage becomes `.ready`, and drop `beginEntry`'s `stage == .ready` early-out on sign-up. At minimum, re-run it when "Check again" moves `.waiting` to `.ready`.
2. **No update screen, no deprecated notice, `/api/client-status` never asked** (rows 17-19). Fix: ask `/api/client-status` at launch; on a 426 or `retired` show a full-screen "Update FAM" with the server's `error` and `update_url`; on `deprecated` say the server's message once. This must ship in 1.0 (IOS_APP.md:211).
3. **No Terms re-acceptance card** (row 79). Fix: after `me()` and every sign-in, read `/api/consent` `terms`; when it is not accepted, show the web's modal (title chosen by `accepted_version`, the box, Continue posting `scope:"terms"`).
4. **An Apple/Google account made from the log-in screen never agrees to the Terms** (row 56). Fix: row 3's card covers it. Alternatively, ask for the terms tick when `is_new` comes back true.
5. **No welcome screen and no rotating "Most played today" sample** (rows 20-24). Fix: a signed-out root with the wordmark, the headline, the 16:9 sample card (4.5 s turn, dots, play/pause in place, "Starting the episode…"), and Sign Up / Log In that open AuthView in each mode.
6. **No FAM intro on launch and no hand-off into DailyFAM** (rows 2-3). Fix: a SwiftUI overlay doing the four beats at the web's timings (fold at 260/420 ms, charge at 700/560 ms, slide at 1320/480 ms), skipped for Reduce Motion.
7. **The waitlist status page is a stub** (rows 81-89). Fix: build from `/api/waitlist/me`: "<First>, you're a Founding FAM member #n", the place card with its sentences and rail, the share link (Copy / Share), Your FAM faces with invite slots, friend search, and "You're in · Open FAM" when `status == active`.
8. **No referral attribution** (rows 58, 93). Fix: accept `referralCode` from a universal link or a pasted code, and send it as `referral_code` on signup and provider calls.
9. **Sign-up is a different product from the web's** (row 26). The web makes a passwordless waitlist join; iOS makes a password account. Fix: the owner decides; then align on `/api/waitlist/join` or document the split.
10. **No `POST /api/voice/wake` on returning to the foreground** (row 8). Fix: on `scenePhase == .active` with a signed-in listener, fire and forget the call.
11. **Tapping the dim records a consent decline** (row 76). Fix: make the backdrop inert, as on the web; only Not now posts `allow:false`.
12. **Linking an Apple ID or Google account that has its own FAM account swaps accounts without clearing anything** (row 57). Fix: when `who.userId != before`, run `forgetEverything()`-style resets, keep the new token, then `beginEntry` and reload the rails.

## Gaps, ranked

1. **MISSING: joining a joint mix.** Nothing calls `joinMix`, and there are no `/j/` universal links, so an invite cannot be accepted on iPhone. Fix: associated domains for `/j/` and `/m/`, then `api.joinMix(token)`, then open the mix with "You're in — add the topics you want to hear".
2. **MISSING: shared `/m/<id>` mix links.** Fix: the same universal-link handler, then `publicMix(id)`, then the public page, or your own page when it is yours.
3. **MISSING: the generation give-up.** There is no 45 s quiet timeout and no `voice_wait` + 15 s voice timeout, with their two sentences, so iOS can wait forever. The playlist's 45-second failure path never fires. Fix: port `holdGenTimeout` into `LoadingModel`, fail into `generationFailed` / `mixItemFailed`.
4. **DIFFERS, bug: the fallback Go Deeper suggestion is sent as the listener's own words.** iOS spell-corrects it, sends `own=1` and asks for consent. The same happens in What's next and the shared-episode Go Deeper. Fix: compute `own` as `typed || words != suggestion` (the suggestion text, not the thread) in `GoDeeperSheet.go`, `NowPlayingModel.followUp` and `playNextUp`.
5. **DIFFERS, bug: the listen time ignores the pinned zone.** iOS sends `TimeZone.current.identifier`. Fix: send `FAMAPI.listenerZone` at MixView.swift:566.
6. **MISSING: the "your mix is ready" push, its subscribe-on-set, and the tap that opens your own mix.** Fix: APNs `kind` on the server, then register on setting a listen time, port the `mixWhenNote` states, and handle the tap by opening the mix.
7. **DIFFERS: the mix cover has no position-and-zoom editor.** iOS centre-crops. Fix: reuse `PhotoEditor` with a square mask, titled "Position your cover", with the note above Save.
8. **DIFFERS: Recommended topics are not topped back up to five after a (+).** Fix: after an add, append the next unseen ids to `shown` until there are five.
9. **MISSING: "See plans" on the limit card.** Fix: add the primary button that opens `PlansSheet`.
10. **DIFFERS, bug: when the last playlist item fails and something is queued, nothing plays.** Fix: in `mixItemFailed`, if `advanceMix()` returns false, call `nowPlaying.playQueueNext()`, or have `finishMix` play the queue as the web does.
11. **DIFFERS: sign-out keeps the speed, pitch lock and pinned time zone.** The web clears `fam.prefs`. Fix: remove "speed", "pitch_lock" and `fam.prefs.time_zone` in `forgetEverything`, and reset the player's rate.
12. **MISSING: the pitch toast in the player's speed sheet.** Fix: say "Speed changes keep the voice's pitch" or "…will shift the voice's pitch" there, as Settings already does.
13. **DIFFERS: source marks' colours and favicons.** Fix: use the web's `% 9973` hash, and take the favicon from `item.url`'s origin, only when there is a URL.
14. **DIFFERS: save from the player is optimistic, drops the server's refusal sentence, and its Undo does nothing once another episode plays.** Fix: light it on success, say `FAMError`'s message, and re-POST the removed episode on Undo whatever is playing.
15. **DIFFERS: accessibility names of the side buttons.** iOS reads "share", "vibe", "save". Fix: "Share this episode", "VIBE! this to your friends" / "Take the vibe back", and "Save for later" / "Remove from Save for Later".
