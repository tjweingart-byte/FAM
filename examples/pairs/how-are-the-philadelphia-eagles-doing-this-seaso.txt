how are the Philadelphia Eagles doing this season

BEFORE:
Their last game was a 19-11 home loss to the Giants on Sunday the eighth of November. That was their seventh straight loss. They opened the season with a win over Washington in September, and they have not won since. Joe Milton made his first start for the team in the Giants game. The losing run before it has been close in some games and not in others: two points down to Jacksonville in October, eleven to Carolina, then seventeen to Dallas and seventeen to Washington. The scoring says more than the record does. Across nine games they have scored 148 points and allowed 221, a deficit of seventy-three. Losing by two points is one kind of season. Losing by seventy-three over nine games means being beaten week after week, not just losing the tight finishes. In the division, the table is plain. The Giants are five wins, three losses and a tie. After that comes Thanksgiving Day in Dallas, then a trip to Arizona on the sixth of December. The next chance to end the losing run is at home, against Pittsburgh.

AFTER:
The Philadelphia Eagles are two and seven, last in the NFC East, and they have lost seven games in a row. They won their first two, over Washington and Tennessee, and have not won since September.

In Jacksonville on the eleventh of October the margin was two points. It has not been that close since. Carolina beat them by eleven, then Dallas and Washington by seventeen each. The most recent was nineteen-eleven to the Giants on Sunday the eighth of November, in Joe Milton's first start for Philadelphia.

That is why the points say more than the record does. Over nine games the Eagles have been outscored by seventy-three. Washington, the next worst in the division, is at minus twenty-seven, and the Giants, who lead it, have scored seven more than they have allowed.

The Eagles did not play this past weekend, because it was their bye. When they come back, on Sunday the twenty-second, it is at home against Pittsburgh - in the stadium where they have won one game in five.
