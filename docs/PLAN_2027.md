# FAM: the plan for 2027

Written on 2026-11-16 from a fourteen-question interview with the owner
(PROBLEMS.md §635). Every number here is labelled: **said** (the owner's
answer), **estimate** (from `docs/FINANCIAL.md`, itself an estimate), or
**derived** (worked out here, with the working shown). Where this plan and
what actually happens disagree, change the plan.

## What you want, in your words

"Listeners is our main focus." You are "confident in the episode quality" -
you have heard about thirty episodes in the last two weeks and have not
stopped one early in a while - so you would open to the public as the app
stands, and the goal is **December 22**. Money is the thing you chose to be
worse at in 2027. By mid-March you want **2,500 consistent weekly
listeners** to show an investor, with revenue projections and a market
size beside it; you have about **five months of runway** and plan to raise
around March. By the end of 2027 you want **25,000 people listening every
week**. What has been in the way is plain: "not enough effort has been put
into outreach", and how many people come back is "definitely something we
want to measure" - today nobody can say.

## Three priorities, and one thing to stop

1. **Reach.** Outreach is the main work from this week, and it is Ian's
   lane with a number on it every week. 200 are on the waitlist (said);
   1,000 by December 22 (said) is 800 more in 36 days, about 22 a day
   (derived).
2. **Coming back.** "Consistent weekly listener" is the number the raise
   and the year rest on, and FAM cannot count it yet. Build the count
   first, then find out from people who left why they left.
3. **Money, in time.** Five months of runway ends in mid-April (derived).
   A round opened in March has about a month to close before that, so the
   preparation starts in January, not March.

**Stop:** shipping changes as a substitute for learning why people leave.
You are not planning new features, which is right. The one you might add -
groups in Messages that work like a Reddit, Facebook or X group - is held
until the return rate is known: the app already has group chats, and a
feed-style group pulls against the project's first rule ("social
information, not social media").

Two things about this table. **Growing 27% every month for nine months is
hard**, and it is what 25,000 means. And **sign-ups are not listeners**: if
one in four sign-ups becomes a weekly listener, 2,500 by March needs about
10,000 sign-ups; if one in two, 5,000 (derived). That ratio is unknown, and
January exists to find it. When it is known, redo this table.

Cost, for scale (estimates, `docs/FINANCIAL.md` and
`docs/SCALING_TIMELINE.md`): about $1,100 a month from launch day ($490 to
run it with the always-on voice, $632 for the sports and markets data), and
about $2,300 a month at 10,000 monthly listeners. There is no checkout and
the paid tiers are off, so there is no revenue in this plan until one is
built.

## Month by month

Each month has one goal, the actions with a name on each, and how you will
know. "You" is the owner (backend).

### November 2026 (the two weeks left): be able to count
- **You:** put one number on `/admin` - people who listened this week and
  also last week - and record which channel each sign-up came from. Neither
  exists today.
- **Ian:** post in fifteen named Facebook, Reddit and Discord communities,
  each logged on `/admin/groups` with its date and what came of it.
- **Brady:** a one-page cost sheet by listener count, and a first list of
  thirty investors to approach.
- **Done when:** the returning-listener number is on `/admin`, and
  sign-ups pass 500 by November 30 (derived: 22 a day).

### December 2026: open on the 22nd with 1,000 signed up
- **You:** let the waitlist in in batches from the 8th, to see the voice
  and the servers under real use before the door opens.
- **You:** decide by the 15th what happens if Apple has not approved the
  app. The web app does not need Apple; opening there on the 22nd keeps
  the date.
- **Ian:** every person let in is asked to send one share link; a shared
  episode plays for anyone.
- **Done when:** the app is open on December 22, 1,000 have signed up, and
  the first week's returning-listener number is written down.

### January 2027: find the ratio
- **You:** from the launch-week sign-ups, how many listened in week two?
  That is the ratio everything else is scaled by. Redo the table above.
- **You and Ian:** twenty conversations with people who tried FAM and did
  not come back. Fix the two reasons heard most.
- **Brady:** first investor conversations. They are for advice now and for
  money in March; a round started cold in March will not close by
  mid-April.
- **Done when:** the ratio is written here, and 800 people are listening
  consistently each week.

### February: the evidence for the raise
- **Ian:** put the effort where the ratio is best. Drop the channel whose
  sign-ups do not become listeners, however many it brings.
- **Brady:** the deck, built on the measured return rate, with the market
  size and projections beside it. Ask active listeners one question
  through the app's survey: would you pay, and how much. A projection with
  nothing behind it will be asked about.
- **You:** fixes only, chosen from what the January conversations found.
- **Done when:** 1,700 consistent weekly listeners, a deck with real
  numbers, and at least ten investor meetings booked for March.

### March: open the round
- **March 1 checkpoint.** You decided this in advance: if the number is
  600 and not 2,500, **you raise anyway on the story, and put more of the
  money raised into new marketing strategies.** Hold to it.
- **Brady:** run the round. Know the answer to "why do people come back"
  and "why did the others not".
- **Added by the interviewer, not decided by you:** write down the bare
  monthly cost FAM could run on if the round slips past April - which data
  plan would be paused first, and what a listener would lose. You may
  never need it. Deciding it in April, with weeks left, is worse.
- **Done when:** 2,500 consistent weekly listeners on March 15 or the
  number that is true, and term conversations under way.

### April: close before the runway ends
- **Brady:** close. Mid-April is where the five months end.
- **You and Ian:** nothing new; keep the weekly number rising while the
  round takes Brady's time.
- **Done when:** money in the bank covers the rest of 2027, or the bare
  cost plan from March is in force. 3,600 by the 30th.

### May: spend on marketing, and measure it
- **Ian:** three new marketing strategies, each with a fixed budget and
  one measure - the cost of one consistent weekly listener. This is what
  you said the money was for.
- **You:** the count on `/admin` split by where a listener came from, so
  a paid channel can be compared with a free one.
- **Done when:** 4,600, and a cost per consistent listener for each
  strategy.

### June: keep what worked, and decide on groups
- **Ian:** double the strategy with the lowest cost per consistent
  listener; stop the one with the highest.
- **All three:** decide on the groups feature now, with half a year of
  evidence on what brings people back. Build it only if listeners have
  asked for it in their own words.
- **Done when:** 5,800, and a yes or no on groups with the reason written
  down.

### July: decide how FAM makes money
- **Brady and you:** the tiers exist in the code and are switched off, and
  there is no checkout. Decide the price and whether to build payment this
  quarter, from February's survey and what investors asked.
- **Done when:** 7,400, and a dated decision on payment.

### August: the second channel
- **Ian:** one channel that is not a community post - a partnership, a
  podcast, a school, a newsletter - tested against the same measure.
- **Done when:** 9,500, and one channel besides communities bringing at
  least a fifth of new consistent listeners.

### September: hold the return rate as it grows
- **You:** compare the return rate of September's new listeners with
  January's. Growth that lowers it is being bought, not earned.
- **Done when:** 12,000, with the return rate no lower than in March.

### October: capacity before the last stretch
- **You:** `docs/SCALING_TIMELINE.md` says what changes at each size.
  Make the changes for 25,000 before the listeners arrive, not after.
- **Done when:** 15,400, and the scaling checklist for the next stage
  done.

### November: one year on
- **All three:** a year after this was written. What in this plan was
  wrong? Write the plan for 2028 from what was learnt, not from this one.
- **Done when:** 19,600.

### December 2027: 25,000
- **Done when:** 25,000 people listened this week and also last week.

## The Sunday check-in

Five questions, answered in writing every Sunday by whoever holds the
number. Paste the answers to Claude with this file if you want them read
back against the plan.

1. How many people listened this week who also listened last week, and
   what was this month's target?
2. How many signed up this week, and from which channel?
3. What did Ian post and where, and which one brought listeners, not only
   sign-ups?
4. What did somebody who stopped listening tell us, and what did we change
   because of it?
5. How many months of runway are left, and did anything ship this week
   that was not a fix or something a listener's behaviour asked for?

- **Whether the three of you agreed 1,000 by December 22**, or whether it
  is one person's number. It should be all three's by Friday.

  is not.
- **Revenue.** Projections are asked for in March and nothing is charged.
