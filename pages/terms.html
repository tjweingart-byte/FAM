Terms and community rules

FAM by Familiarize

FAM turns what you ask and what you follow into short audio stories, and lets you share them, comment on them and talk about them with other people. By creating an account or using FAM you agree to these terms.

Zero tolerance

There is no place on FAM for objectionable content or abusive people. You agree not to post, send, caption or search for anything that:

  • harasses, bullies, threatens or intimidates anyone;
  • attacks people for who they are, including race, ethnicity, religion, disability, sex, gender identity or sexual orientation;
  • is sexual, or involves a minor in any way that is sexual or harmful;
  • promotes violence, self-harm or terrorism;
  • is spam, a scam, or impersonates someone else;
  • shares someone's private information without their permission.

Reporting and blocking

Every comment, message, vibe, profile and episode has a Report option in its menu. Reported content disappears for you straight away, and a person reviews every report within {{REVIEW_HOURS}} hours. When a report is upheld we remove the content, and we suspend the account that posted it.

You can block anyone from their profile or a conversation. Neither of you will see the other's comments, messages, vibes, profile or searches, and neither can message or follow the other. They aren't told. You can unblock people in Settings.

Episodes and AI

FAM's episodes are written by AI from the sources it finds, which are shown on the player. They can be wrong. Don't rely on an episode for medical, legal, financial or safety decisions. When you search, FAM asks before sending what you type, say or attach to its AI provider, Anthropic, and a search drawn from it to Exa; you can change your answer in Settings. The privacy policy says what is kept and who receives it.

Your account

You can delete your account at any time in Settings. Deleting it removes what FAM keeps about you; episodes you searched stay for other listeners without your name. We may suspend or remove accounts that break these rules.
