Privacy policy

FAM by Familiarize · updated {{UPDATED}}

FAM turns what you ask and what you follow into short audio stories. This policy says what FAM keeps about you, who else receives any of it and why, how long it is kept, and how to change or delete it. "FAM", "we" and "us" mean Familiarize, which runs FAM.

The short version

  • We keep what FAM needs to work: your account, what you ask and listen to (to make your feeds), and what you post.
  • What you ask is sent to Anthropic to write the episode, and a search drawn from it to Exa to find sources, only after you say yes in the app. Anthropic does not use it to train its models.
  • We show no ads, sell nothing, and track you across no other apps or sites.
  • You can delete your account at any time in Settings, and it is gone straight away.

What other people can see

  • Your profile: name, @handle, picture, the interests you choose to show, the place you set, your public mixes and your vibes.
  • Comments can be read by anyone; messages only by the people in the conversation.
  • Mixes are public when you make them; you can make one private.
  • Episodes you search can be played by other listeners in Explore, without your name unless you turn on "Show my name on episodes I searched". Questions that look personal (with "my", an email address or a phone number) and anything with an attachment are never shared.

Your choices and rights

  • Change your profile, place, interests and settings in the app.
  • Turn Send my questions to AI on or off in Settings, Privacy and safety.
  • Block anyone; report anything.
  • Delete your account in Settings. It cannot be undone.
  • Ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, by emailing {{EMAIL_LINK}}. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California) these are legal rights, and you may also complain to your data protection authority. We do not sell or share personal information as California law defines it.

Cookies and storage on your device

FAM uses one cookie to keep you signed in. Your device also keeps your playback settings and, so they play without a connection, episodes you have finished. Signing out clears them. There are no advertising or analytics cookies.

Security

Passwords and sign-in tokens are stored only as one-way hashes, and every connection to FAM is encrypted. No system is perfectly secure; if something goes wrong that affects you, we will tell you.

Children

FAM is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has an account, email us and we will delete it.

Where your information is processed

FAM and the companies above process information in the United States and other countries, which may have different data protection laws from yours.

Changes

We will update the date above when this policy changes, and tell you in the app before a change in what is sent to a third party takes effect.
