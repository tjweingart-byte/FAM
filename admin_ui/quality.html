Episode quality

Every written episode, scored on six things and filed by the category tree (PROBLEMS.md §306). Code scores are proxies measured on every episode, 0 to 1. Grade is the model grader's, shown but not used for ranking until it agrees with your own scores. Listeners is what people did: for the hook, the share who stayed past twenty seconds; for storytelling, the share who finished. Nodes are sorted by episodes × how far below their own heading they fall.

Your scores against the grader's, per metric. A metric's grades count once they land within one point of yours on enough episodes. Score episodes from any node's weakest list.

Does each measure predict listening?

Rank correlation between each measure and how much of an episode its listeners heard. One that predicts nothing is a measure to reword, not a number to optimise.
