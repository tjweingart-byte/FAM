Communities to reach

Facebook groups, subreddits, X Communities, Discord servers and Slack communities about the subjects FAM covers, found by web search and Reddit's and X's own APIs, or sent to Egan by you. Each card says how to reach the admins through the community's own public channel, three episodes FAM could make for its members (ideas only, not written), and a first message to edit. You reach out from FAM's account; nothing here sends anything. FAM never collects phone numbers, and keeps an email only when the community publishes it as its contact.

Send a group to Egan
Egan looks it up, works out the public way to its admins, judges the fit and drafts a message. It stays in To contact whatever he thinks of it.
