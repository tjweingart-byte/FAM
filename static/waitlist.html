Our story

We believe information can bring people closer.

The idea for FAM grew out of summers we spent living and working together in the forest, where we experienced firsthand how meaningful human connection can be. Coming back to everyday life, we noticed how fragmented everything felt. Our attention, our information, and sometimes even our relationships.

We began to realize that information is one of the ways we understand each other. What someone is curious about, what they’re learning, and what fascinates them tells us something about who they are. And when we share those things, we create more opportunities to connect.

We started FAM with a simple idea: what if the information you cared about could come to you? What began as a way to make information more accessible through personalized audio grew into something bigger.

Today, we’re building FAM around the belief that our information worlds don’t have to exist separately. By bringing together what we know, what we’re learning, and what we’re curious about, we can create more shared understanding and bring people closer together.

Brady Weingart, Ian Solomon & TJ Weingart Founders
