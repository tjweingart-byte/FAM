Why we built FAM

Why should someone else decide which stories you hear?

FAM began inside a podcast company, where a handful of people decided which stories became episodes and ultimately controlled what listeners heard.
That raised a question: why should you wait for someone else to make an episode about the things you care about?

For most of the history of media, someone had to
  1. Research it
  2. Write it
  3. Record it
  4. Edit it
  5. Publish it
Now the technology exists to collapse all of that

You ask. FAM tells you the story.

That’s why we built FAM.

No more
  • Searching, then reading through articles
  • An hour-long podcast for the ten minutes you care about
  • Accepting that you’re going to miss things
FAM gives you
  • The things you care about, told as a story
  • Episodes that fit the time you have
  • Enough to join the conversation
How to get the most out of FAM

Search. Scroll. Mix.

You choose how you listen. Three ways in, one thing in common: every episode is yours.

01 · Search

Ask for exactly the episode you want.

Type or say your question, pick how long you want to listen, and FAM creates a custom episode just for you.

  • Prompt it like you’d ask a friend who knows everything
  • Choose your length, from a quick minute to a deep dive
  • Go deeper with a follow-up when it ends
Your episode, made for you
02 · DailyFAM

A page that refreshes all day long.

DailyFAM is always full of relevant, up-to-date episodes, so there’s something worth hearing every time you open it.

  • Made for you from what you love to hear about
  • Trending stories from around the world
  • What you missed while you were busy
  • What’s hot on FAM and with your friends
The latest, all in one place
03 · myFAM

Your topics, mixed and played in one tap.

myFAM is the easiest way to stay up to date. Build custom playlists, called mixes, from the topics you follow, and get the latest on them every day.

  • Want a daily recap of your team or the markets? Add them to a mix
  • One tap plays through all the latest, back to back
  • Share your mixes, or copy one somebody else made
Your mix, your way, made daily
